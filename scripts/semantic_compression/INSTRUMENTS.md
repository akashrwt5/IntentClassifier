Generated by `inventory_instruments.py --write`. Do not edit by hand: every
number below is measured, and a hand-edit is how a charter starts describing
a file that no longer exists in that shape.

Measured 2026-08-23 against `language_packs/en/train.csv` (8,430 rows, 57 intents).

**Exact leak** is the share of rows whose normalised text already appears in
training data. **Near-dup** adds rows within token-set Jaccard 0.8 of a training
utterance. **MDE** is the smallest accuracy difference McNemar's test can resolve
at 15% discordance, 80% power -- a result smaller than this is not a
small win, it is a number the instrument cannot tell from zero.

## Charters

**`dev_hard.csv`** — 813 rows, 57 intents

PRIMARY DECISION INSTRUMENT (P2-P8). Holdout rows with no near-duplicate in train.csv. Decide with McNemar on discordant items, never by comparing two accuracy numbers. Frozen for the duration of the plan.

**`dev_near.csv`** — 657 rows, 52 intents

REGRESSION DETECTION ONLY. A high score is evidence of memorisation, not of generalisation. Never gate on it; never report it without dev_hard beside it.

**`holdout_honest.csv`** — 1,470 rows, 57 intents

The parent of the two above, kept for continuity with earlier numbers. Report it only to compare against history; decide on dev_hard.

**`holdout_leakage_guard.csv`** — 331 rows, 57 intents

Broad, clean, independent of the train/holdout split. The best available second opinion on dev_hard, and the natural cross-check when a dev_hard result is close to its MDE.

**`extras/semantic_holdout_2.csv`** — 341 rows, 59 intents

Broad and clean. Same role as the leakage guard. Carries two malformed labels (see the defect note below) which must be excluded before scoring.

**`extras/holdout_paraphrase.csv`** — 100 rows, 10 intents

Deep paraphrases on a narrow slice of the taxonomy. Strong evidence about those intents, silent about the rest -- never read as an overall figure.

**`extras/benchmark_250.csv`** — 249 rows, 10 intents

Historical benchmark. Heavily overlapped with training data and narrow in taxonomy: a memorisation measure. Do not use it to compare encoders.

**`extras/oos.csv`** — 156 rows, 1 intents

Out-of-scope inputs: measures false-accept rate, not accuracy.

**`extras/oos_2.csv`** — 367 rows, 1 intents

Larger out-of-scope set. Same charter as oos.csv.

## What the measurements say

- `extras/semantic_holdout_2.csv` uses 2 label(s) absent from the training taxonomy: `Cmd.SendMessage - no`, `Cmd.SendMessage - yes`. Rows carrying them are unscoreable — every model is wrong on them by construction.
- `extras/benchmark_250.csv` is **86% exact-leaked** into `train.csv`. Accuracy on it is close to a memorisation score; it cannot rank two encoders.
- The two broad clean sets — `holdout_leakage_guard.csv` and `extras/semantic_holdout_2.csv` — are independent of the train/holdout split and of each other's construction. They are the natural cross-check whenever a `dev_hard` result lands near its MDE.
